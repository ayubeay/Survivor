# Survivor v0.1 — Pilot Recruitment Messages

## Message 1: The Cold-ish DM (for someone you know from crypto Twitter/Telegram)

---

Hey [name] — quick question.

You ever pay someone in USDC for work and get ghosted? Or deliver something and have to chase payment for weeks?

I built something that fixes this. It's called Survivor — basically escrow + automatic dispute resolution for USDC payments.

How it works:
- Payer locks USDC in a smart contract
- If work gets delivered, funds release automatically
- If something goes wrong, the system pauses funds, collects proof from both sides, and resolves it — including fair splits
- 48h challenge window so nobody gets rugged by the system either
- If everything breaks, payer can reclaim after 72h (dead man's switch)

I'm running a small pilot — one real deal, $100-200 USDC on Solana. Looking for someone who either:
- Pays contractors/operators in USDC regularly
- Or does work paid in USDC and wants protection

Want to be the first to test it? I'll walk you through the whole thing. Takes 10 minutes to set up.

## What NOT to say in the recruitment message:

- Don't say "AI agents" — say "automatic resolution"
- Don't say "blockchain infrastructure" — say "USDC escrow"
- Don't say "risk scoring engine" — say "the system evaluates proof"
- Don't say "Solana program" — say "smart contract"
- Don't mention YC, fundraising, or "startup" — you're solving a problem, not pitching
- Don't oversell — "I built something, want to test it?" is stronger than a pitch deck

## Ideal pilot candidate profile:

- Already pays or gets paid in USDC (no education needed)
- Has been burned by a payment dispute at least once
- Crypto-native (understands wallets, on-chain)
- Active on Twitter or Telegram (will talk about the experience)
- Ideally runs a small team or hires contractors regularly
- NOT a close friend who will go easy on you — you want honest friction
